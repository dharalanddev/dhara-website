Why we exist

Most hill land is sold on promises.

Unapproved layouts. Agricultural land sold as “villa plots.” Titles that unravel at the registrar's office.

The Nilgiris is one of India's most desirable places to own land — and one of the easiest places to buy it wrong. Dhara exists because buying land here should feel as solid as the mountains themselves.

The Dhara standard

Three things every Dhara layout is built on.

01

Individual patta, in your name

Each plot in a Dhara layout is subdivided and registered to you with its own patta — not an undivided share, not a paper promise.

02

DTCP-sanctioned layouts

Roads, drainage and open space planned to Tamil Nadu's layout standards and handed over the way the rules require.

03

Verified before we buy

Thirty-year encumbrance search, legal opinion and ground verification — done before land enters our pipeline, not after you ask.
