Privacy

How we handle your details.

Short and plain, because it should be.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, phone number, country, and the preferences and message you choose to share.

Why

To reply to your enquiry and, if you joined the waitlist, to tell you about Dhara projects. That's all.

Where it's kept

Your details are stored in our customer records system (Zoho CRM), accessible only to Dhara's partners. We don't sell or share them with anyone else.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Write to info@dharalanddev.com and we'll act on it promptly.
